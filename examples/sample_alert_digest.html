How to read this

The risk index (0 to 100) combines how far each of an entity's signals has moved from its own normal range, using only data up to the date of this digest. Watch means look; Warning means prepare; Critical means act. An alert is evidence that something has changed, not proof of a disruption: some alerts are false alarms. Exposure is the yearly spend and the SKUs behind an entity, not a forecast of loss. Suggested actions are rule-based starting points.
